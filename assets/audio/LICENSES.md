## Processing and remaining synthesized sounds

Exact excerpt boundaries and processing are in assets/audio/foley/processing.json. New source downloads are retained outside the site in the Codex task work/sources folder. Neon original: https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/sb_neon_nomelody.mp3

Curtain fabric/rings, VHS start/eject and the very quiet window/corridor/TV backgrounds are generated in code; they are not field recordings. The prior procedural sounds remain as fallback if a sample cannot load. No external recordings are attributed to these generated effects.

Listening on real headphones and speakers remains a user audition step; technical checks are not a subjective mixing review.
